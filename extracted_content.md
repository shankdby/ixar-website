# Slide 60 (Slide 60)

- Cooling tower inspection using ROV (For Example)
- Cooling tower sumps are confined underwater environments where conventional inspection methods often involve significant safety risks and operational challenges. Replacing manual diver inspections with an Underwater ROV provided a safer, faster, and more reliable inspection solution. The robotic approach eliminated direct personnel exposure to hazardous confined spaces while delivering accurate inspection data to support maintenance planning and asset integrity management.
- Project Significance
- Industry: EnergyApplication: Cooling Tower Sump InspectionTechnology Used: Underwater ROVInspection Type: Visual InspectionProject Duration: 2 Months
- Project Overview
- IXAR Robotic Solutions successfully completed the underwater inspection of more than 20 cooling tower sumps over a period of two months using an advanced Underwater ROV. The objective was to assess the internal condition of each sump without interrupting plant operations or draining the structures. All inspections were carried out in accordance with the refinery's safety regulations and operational procedures.
