Who We Are

We at IXAR Robotic Solutions, an IIT Bombay and IIT Madras alumni startup, provide submersible robotic inspection and survey solutions using Remotely Operated Vehicles (ROVs). We're a growing team of young tech enthusiasts who design and build robots from scratch to go deep into water bodies — replacing divers and completing the same inspection with better quality, in much less time.

To provide high-quality, made-in-India underwater ROVs for inspection and top-notch survey of submersible structures. Project Overview

IXAR Robotic Solutions successfully completed the underwater inspection of more than 20 cooling tower sumps over a period of two months using an advanced Underwater ROV. The objective was to assess the internal condition of each sump without interrupting plant operations or draining the structures. All inspections were carried out in accordance with the refinery's safety regulations and operational procedures.

The ROV was deployed through the existing manway, enabling rapid and safe access to submerged confined spaces while eliminating the need for diver intervention. A contingency plan for emergency retrieval was implemented throughout the project to ensure operational safety.

The inspection included high-resolution visual assessment, geotagged video recording, defect identification, defect measurement, and comprehensive condition assessment. Upon completion, a detailed inspection report with engineering observations and condition assessment was submitted to the client.
